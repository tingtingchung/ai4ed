# AI in Business Education

An interactive overview of four research projects on AI in business education:

- **Good With It, Worse Without It** — two classroom studies of a commercial LLM learning mode (EAAI)
- **Evolving Faculty Use of Generative AI and Concerns about Academic Integrity** — a two-wave faculty panel (2026 Pre-ICIS SIGDSA)
- **Agents for Business** — a team-based book project for teaching agentic AI (2026 Pre-ICIS SIGDSA)
- **Grant Central Station** — a customizable AI app for grant discovery and proposals (2026 Pre-ICIS SIGDSA)

Live site: https://tingtingchung.github.io/ai4ed/

One self-contained `index.html`, no build step. Figures come from the papers; sample data is marked on the page.
