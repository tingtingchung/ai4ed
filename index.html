Learning with AI · Lyu, Zhang & Chung · EAAI, accepted

A learning mode helped students finish the worksheet, and left them worse off without it

Graduate business analytics students learned neural networks with Gemini's Guided Learning, instructor-led teaching, or Gemini's general mode. Same worksheet, same 40 minutes, then a post-test with no devices.

Teaching with AI · Schlosser, Chung & Zhang · 2026 Pre-ICIS SIGDSA, research-in-progress

Faculty who used generative AI became less worried about academic integrity a year later

Surveys of faculty at a mid-sized Mid-Atlantic university in spring 2024 (n = 204) and fall 2025 (n = 276); 91 answered both. Diffusion of innovations as a process: experience at each stage reshapes the next.

Unstandardized estimates. Indirect effects: half-longitudinal a × b, bias-corrected 95% bootstrap CIs (5,000 resamples).

Build familiarity early

Familiarity predicted later use, which then lowered integrity concerns.

Sustain engagement

Recalibration needed accumulated experience; the same-year path was not significant. Favor ongoing support over one-time workshops.

Reach high-concern, low-use faculty

Concerns constrained use at the same time, not later: no avoidance loop was found.

Building agents · Lawal & Chung · 2026 Pre-ICIS SIGDSA, teaching track

MBA teams built agentic AI for their own business functions, and the class turned it into a book

Instead of only prompting AI, students designed callable tools and the orchestration logic around them, one chapter per function, unified by an instructor and a student co-editor into Agents for Business.

Corporate valuationProcurement & supplier evaluationSupply chain disruptionHR screening & workforce planningProject risk trackingCustomer support triageWinery operations
1 · Core insightWhy a raw LLM is not enough
2 · Tool designDiscrete, independently testable functions
3 · Agent in actionAn orchestrated run on a realistic query
4 · Reasoning made visibleInspect rather than trust

The corporate valuation chapter: 12 tools, 2 agents

Built with the smolagents CodeAgent framework: each tool is a typed Python function with a docstring telling the agent when to call it, drawing live data rather than model guesses.

"Is this company fairly priced?"

Illustrative memo text for a fictional company. The chapter's point: an LLM can hallucinate financials, which gives students a concrete reason to build grounded tools.

Building for nonprofits · Pawar, Muthuraj & Chung · 2026 Pre-ICIS SIGDSA, prototype track

A free, customizable app that finds grants and drafts proposals, built with a nonprofit partner

The partner could only apply to fewer than 10 grants a year and could not afford commercial search subscriptions. Grant Central Station consolidates discovery and drafting, runs on local open-source models, and scores fit deterministically instead of asking an LLM to judge.

Re-weight the fit score

Seven factors, each scored 0–10. Drag a weight and watch the shortlist re-rank. Weights are renormalized to sum to 1.

    Six sample grants invented for this demo. In the paper's test on 59 real grants from three searches, the top five held across large weight changes: goal alignment could take any value from 0.07 to 0.38 with the same top five.
